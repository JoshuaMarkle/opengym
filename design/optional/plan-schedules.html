Plans with a schedule

The Plans tab works as it does today, with one addition: a folder can have a schedule. Either a repeating cycle (PPLR, Upper / Lower / Rest…) or a weekly split where you assign days. Example plan: PPL on a weekly split, Sun Rest · Mon Pull A · Tue Legs · Wed Rest · Thu Push A · Fri Push B · Sat Rest. Today is Thursday, Nov 5.

C3Missed a day

A quiet orange note on Home the day after, never a guilt screen. "Do it today" moves it into today; "Skip it" marks it missed. With "Wait for me" (cycles) there's no note: the missed workout simply stays next.
