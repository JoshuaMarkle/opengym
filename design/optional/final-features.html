Plate pad, supersets and cardio, finished

Your feedback applied: the plate pad is the side-by-side layout with the total on top, a bar that slides left when it fills up, and → / ✓ keys on both keypads. Supersets are as approved. The keypad's → and ✓ are now one Next / Done button. Cardio: no intervals, no Previous column, Σ only with 2+ segments, and progress measured in amount, with race-distance records.
