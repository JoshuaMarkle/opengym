Routines: build, organize, start

Part 1 is the routine builder (T1's look, T2's set rows) and the new exercise picker. Part 2 shows how PL3's folders work. Part 3 is the screen you get when you tap a routine, with 3 versions.

✓ Builder: flat list, no cards. Every exercise shows Set · LBS · REPS rows. Rep ranges are optional, entered with the same keypad.
✓ Picker: exercises already in the routine come first (untick to remove), then the ones you've added. Ticking in A–Z moves an exercise to "Added".
− Removed: "Save & start" (Save is top right) and T4. + The +weight hint is planned for later.

B5Later: +weight hint

Only for exercises with a rep range. If you hit the top of the range on every working set, a hint suggests adding one weight step (5 lb). "Use" fills it in, shown in green. Ignore it and nothing changes. The reps cell shows the range until you type a number.

Things to decide
• Routine detail: R1, R2 or R3? A natural path: ship R1 now and switch to R3 once the +weight hints exist.
• New exercise defaults: when added from the picker, should an exercise start with 3 empty sets, or copy the set layout from the last routine it was in?
• Saving last weights: should "last" stay "last" forever, or can you type a fixed weight into a routine (like the 95 lb warm-up)? Both are shown; you can mix them.
• Picker during a workout: use the same three sections? "In this routine" would become "In this workout".
• Deleting a routine: history is always kept. Should there also be an "Archive routine" option, or are folders enough?
• Today tab: still open from last time. With Plans + detail + Start, it may not be needed.
