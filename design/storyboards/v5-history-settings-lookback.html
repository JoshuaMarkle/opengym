History, Settings, and a look-back while you lift

Part 1 is the in-workout pop-up: one tap from any exercise to see its history, charts and records. Part 2 is the History tab and Part 3 is Settings, each with 3 versions to choose from.

✓ Decided: the suggested combination. Progress opens on G4 cards → G1/G2/G3 per lift → G5 on Finish.
− Removed: G7 (% change) and everything volume (G6, the volume button and the volume stat on the finish screen).
+ New: an in-workout look-back pop-up, History tab versions and Settings versions.
PART 1

In-workout pop-up: History · Charts · Records

A chart button next to "•••" on the exercise. It opens a sheet over about 90% of the screen; swipe down or tap ✕ to go back to your sets. It always opens on the exercise you're logging, and remembers the last tab you used.

Things to decide for the pop-up
• Should the pop-up open on History every time, or on the last tab you used? (Shown: last used.)
• Show a "Today's target" suggestion on Charts, or keep it purely about the past?
• In History, should tapping a past session copy its numbers into today's sets? It would be handy, but easy to tap by accident.
• Should the History tab include all variations (Flat, Incline, DB) with a label, or only the one you're on? (Shown: all, labelled.)

H4Workout detail

Opened from any history version. Every set, drops included, with "best" marked per exercise. "Repeat this workout" starts a new session with these numbers filled in. "Edit" fixes typos; "•••" has delete.

Things to decide for History
• Which list: H1 cards, H2 calendar, H3 compact list, or a mix? A common mix is H3's list with a small calendar icon in the corner that switches to H2.
• Should search (in H3) be there from day one, or added later?
• Should a workout that's still in progress show at the top of History, or only once you finish it?
• Does "Repeat this workout" belong in History, or only in Plans?

S3Backup first

A backup status card at the top (it turns orange after 7 days), then quick-change tiles for the common settings, and a short list for the rest. Protects your data, which matters most when nothing is in the cloud. Less conventional.

Things to decide for Settings
• Which layout: S1, S2, S3, or a mix? A natural mix is S3's backup card on top of S2's inline controls.
• How should backup work? (a) A manual "Back up now" that saves a file to the Files app, (b) a reminder every X days, or (c) automatic sync to your server later. One option is a + b now and c later.
• Which options belong on the main Settings screen and which go one level deeper? Candidates for the deeper level: 1RM formula, pinned lifts, available plates, first day of week.
• Should Settings have an exercise library editor (rename, merge, link variations), or should that live in Plans?
