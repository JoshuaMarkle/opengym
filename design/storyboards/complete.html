★ · DESIGN PRINCIPLES

How the app should feel, and the rules that get it there

When a new screen or feature comes up, it should pass all ten.

1Logging is the product

Every other screen exists to make logging faster. One tap completes a set; weights and reps come pre-filled from last time; typing is the exception.

2The plan is a guide, not a script

Do exercises in any order, add a drop set any time, swap Bench for Incline, add or remove exercises mid-workout. The app adapts and asks at the end whether to keep the changes.

3Quiet, flat, black and white

Pure black (or white) background, hairline dividers, no cards, no shadows, no gradients. The interface steps back so the numbers stand out.

4Color always means something

Blue = action / done. Green = improvement or records. Orange = drop sets and warnings. Red = delete or decline. Never decoration, never the only signal (always paired with text or a symbol).

5Numbers first

Weights and reps are the biggest, boldest things on screen, in tabular figures so columns line up. "185 × 8" is the standard way to write a set.

6Gestures are shortcuts, never the only way

Swipe right = warm-up, swipe left = delete, press-and-hold = options. Everything a gesture does is also reachable from a visible button or menu.

7Thumbs at the bottom, decisions at the top

Things you do constantly (✓, + Set, Prev / Next, keypad, Start) live in the bottom half. Save / Done sit top right, Cancel / Back top left, the same on every screen.

8Forgiving

Undo after deletes, confirmation before anything that loses logged work, and typing required for the one truly irreversible action (Delete all data).

9Your data, on your phone

No account, no server, works offline. Because of that, backup is a first-class feature: visible date, reminders, one-tap export, and imports from Strong / Hevy.

10No bloat

No rest timer, goals, volume charts, social feed, training videos, diet plans, ads or subscriptions. If a feature doesn't help you log or see progress, it isn't here.

CONVENTIONS

Sets: 185 × 8 · BW × 12 · +25 × 8 · −40 × 10
Dates: Thu, Oct 29 · relative within a week ("Mon")
Units: lbs default, "lb" after numbers
Spacing: 16 px edges, 8 px grid, 44 px min tap
Swipe → warm-up · Swipe ← delete · Hold = menu
Top bar: Cancel / ‹ Back left · Save / Done right
Sheets for options; alerts only to confirm loss
Deliberately not in this version: goals · +weight hints after hitting the top of a rep range · rest timer · volume / tonnage charts · cardio and timed exercises · social anything · weekly schedule. Rep ranges are already stored, so +weight hints can be added later without changing any data.
