History ranges, Settings, Plans and the routine builder

Part 1 applies your History, Settings and pop-up changes. Part 2 is new: versions of the Plans tab, and of what building a routine (a template of exercises) looks like.

✓ History: calendar by default, a toggle that switches to the compact list, and Month / 6M / Year / All ranges.
✓ Settings: S1 layout with S2's data section and a thinner toggle.
✓ Pop-up History tab: minimal rows with the H3 date block; best set and est. 1RM removed.

Open questions from Part 1
• Year view: mini months (shown) or a single GitHub-style grid of squares? The squares would be too small at phone width, so I went with months.
• All time: monthly bars (shown), or just a yearly total per row?
• Backup reminder: should the header turn orange after 7 days without a backup, like S3 did?
• Calendar colors: one blue for every workout (shown), or a different shade per routine (Push / Pull / Legs)?

Things to decide
• Plans layout: PL1 cards, PL2 week schedule, PL3 folders, or a mix (e.g. PL3 folders with PL1's Start button on each row)?
• Weekly schedule: do you want the app to know your week (PL2), or just pick a routine each time?
• Builder: T1 summary list, T2 set tables, or T3 just set counts? T1 + T4 covers the most with the least clutter.
• Rep ranges: needed at all? If yes, should the app suggest adding weight when you hit the top of the range on every set?
• Editing a routine mid-workout (from the exercise list): should changes save back to the routine, or only apply today? Ask each time?
• Today tab: with Plans handling routines, is a separate Today tab still needed, or should the app open on Plans?
