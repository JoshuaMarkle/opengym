Six versions of the Home screen

Each one gets you into a workout in one tap and offers a blank workout. They differ in what fills the rest of the screen. Same data throughout: it's Thursday, Nov 5; you did Pull A on Monday and Legs on Tuesday, and Push A is next in your PPL folder. The tab is renamed "Home".

− Out for now: goals, +weight hints (B5 / R3 style targets).
✓ Bodyweight is a setting on each exercise (type: weight, bodyweight, BW + added, assisted).
✓ Routine screen: R2's full set preview plus a Previous column when there's history. Look: flat rows and hairlines, no cards.

Home: 6 versions

"Next up" in each comes from your active folder's rotation: the routine done longest ago goes next. Tapping a routine's name opens its routine screen; tapping Start or ▶ starts it immediately.

Things to decide
• Which version, or which parts to combine? Some natural mixes: F with E's in-progress block taking the top while a workout is running; B's minimalism plus D's checklist underneath; C's 4-week grid in place of F's week strip.
• "Next up" logic: the routine done longest ago in the active folder (shown), or strict folder order?
• Resume bar: while a workout is running, a thin "Push A · 32:18 · Resume ›" bar above the tabs on every screen (not just Home). Use it?
• Streak definition: weeks in a row with at least N workouts. What's N, or should the streak just be "weeks with any workout"?
• Two workouts in one day (E): needed, or is one workout a day the rule?
