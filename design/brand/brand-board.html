App icon, logo and icon set

Six logo directions on a black background (dark is the default; each has a light version), shown at App Store size, Home Screen size (60) and Settings size (29). Then two icon styles, outline and solid, for the tab bar and every in-app control. All SVGs are in design/brand/.

To decide
• Logo: which of L1–L6 (or a combination, e.g. the L3 wordmark with the L2 plate)?
• Icon style: A outline or B solid? A suits the flat hairline look; B is easier to read in the gym. A common mix: outline for inactive tabs, solid for the active one.
• W / D badges: keep the letters (clear, already designed) or switch to the flame / step icons?
