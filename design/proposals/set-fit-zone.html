Strength estimate per set: e1RM = lbs × (1 + (reps + RIR) / 30). Same Epley formula as the logic doc, with RIR added to the reps.

Lbs × reps for set 1

Each curve shows every lbs × reps pair at the same strength for one RIR. The shaded area is where the curves for your target RIRs cross your rep range. Any weight under the shading lands in range with a target effort. The ring is what you logged, and the diamond is the suggested weight for next time.

Third dimension: RIR as slices

Each row is one RIR. The bar is the band of weights that keeps you in the rep range at that effort. More reps in reserve slides the band to lighter weights. Stacking these rows gives the 3D shape you described, and the target RIRs are the rows in the shaded zone above.

Per set suggestion

Rule: if the logged set is already in range at a target RIR, keep the weight. Otherwise pick the weight that puts the middle of the rep range at the middle target RIR, rounded to the step. That gives up, keep or drop for each set on its own.
