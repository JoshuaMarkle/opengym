RPE and RIR

An optional effort rating per set, shown as a badge on the lower right of the reps box (no new column), entered from a compact effort pad (two rows of normal keys) with ½ for half steps.

Data: WorkoutSet.rpe?: number (1–10, step 0.5), RoutineSet.rpeTarget?: number, Settings.effort: 'off'|'rpe'|'rir', Settings.effortAsk: 'every'|'last'|'tap', Settings.effortIn1rm: boolean. RIR is display only (10 − RPE; 5+ means RPE ≤ 5). Badge text: RPE shows the number (8, 8.5). RIR adds R (2R, 1.5R); a half drops the R when the box is too narrow (1.5). CSV export adds an RPE column. Est. 1RM with effort: Epley on reps + RIR.
A · EFFORT

RPE and RIR as a badge on the reps box

No new column. Effort is a small badge clipped to the lower right of the reps box, so the table keeps its current layout. It is entered from the keypad: on a reps box the field tabs read lbs · reps · rpe, and the rpe tab swaps the number keys for an effort pad (same spot as the plate pad): six keys labelled Easy to Max, plus ½ for half steps. It is shorter than the number keypad, so the table gains a row of space. With "Ask on: every set", Next on reps goes to the effort pad first, then Done. Stored once as WorkoutSet.rpe (6–10, half steps); RIR is shown as 10 − RPE, so switching modes never loses data.

A6History and progress

Each set is one unbreakable block (185 × 8 @8). When a line runs out of room the whole block wraps, and no dot is left hanging at the end of a line. With "Use effort in est. 1RM" on, a set at @8 counts as 2 reps short of failure.
