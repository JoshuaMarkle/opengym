A · DAY PAGE

Meals fold, and the right one is open

Each meal is a folder row: chevron, name, number of foods, total kcal. Tapping the row opens or closes it; nothing else happens on that tap. When the page opens, the meal for the time of day is open and the rest are closed: Breakfast until 11:00, Lunch until 4:00 pm, Dinner after that. Snacks never open by themselves. Whatever you open or close by hand stays that way until the next meal window starts. A meal with nothing logged shows + Add under its row, open or closed.

F · FOOD INSPECTOR

Looking at one food

Opened by tapping a logged food. The top is a ring card: calories in the middle, the ring split by where those calories come from, each segment with rounded ends and a small gap. Beside it, protein · carbs · fat with the share in its color, grams large, and the name under. Under that, the full nutrients with % of daily value. F1 was the pick; F2 and F3 are dropped. The same card is used on C2, C3, D4 and S4.

S · SETUP

About you, goal, pace, result

First open of the Food tab, and later from Targets → Recalculate. Four steps with a thin step bar at the top. Numbers are typed on the usual keypad. Estimates use Mifflin-St Jeor for resting calories, times an activity factor that takes how often you lift from your history.

T · TARGETS

One Targets screen: ring on top, diets below

One calorie target, then the split. Diets: Lifter (default) 28 / 45 / 27 · Balanced 30 / 40 / 30 · High protein 40 / 35 / 25 · Low carb 35 / 25 / 40 · Keto 25 / 5 / 70 · Endurance 20 / 55 / 25 · Custom. Shares are of calories (protein / carbs / fat); grams follow from 2,700 kcal.

C · ADD FOOD

Find it, set the amount, log it

C1 is unchanged except the colors. C2 has a short subtitle, the ring card instead of the four numbers, and no "After this" line. C3 uses the ring card in its result sheet.

D · ADD FOOD, OTHER WAYS

Saved meals and the Meals tab

D4 uses the ring card for the meal's totals. D5: tapping + logs the meal right away and the button turns into a blue ✓; tapping ✓ takes it back off and returns to +. No bottom button. A short toast confirms, with Undo.

E · RULES

What changed in the rules

Meal foldersBreakfast · Lunch · Dinner · Snacks. Auto-open windows: Breakfast before 11:00, Lunch 11:00–4:00 pm, Dinner from 4:00 pm. Snacks only open by hand. Open/closed state is kept per day until the next window starts. Past days open with every meal closed. Empty meals always show + Add.
SetupSex (for the estimate only), age, height, weight, daily activity. Lifting days per week come from the last 4 weeks of history. Goal: Lose fat · Maintain · Build muscle · Just log. Pace: lose 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 / 1.5 lb a week (−125 / −250 / −500 / −750 kcal), gain 0.25 / 0.5 lb a week (+125 / +250). A goal weight is optional and only used for the date estimate.
Split presetsLifter (default): protein 1 g per lb of bodyweight, fat 27%, carbs the rest. Others are fixed shares of calories. Custom keeps the last numbers typed. Shares always add to 100%; changing one moves carbs unless carbs is the one changed, then fat moves.
Ring cardSegments are each macro's share of calories (4 / 4 / 9 kcal per g), in protein → carbs → fat order clockwise from the top. Round caps, 3 px visual gap. A macro at 0 g has no segment. The center is kcal for the amount shown.
Meals tab+ logs the whole meal into the chosen meal slot at once (each food as its own row). ✓ on the same screen removes exactly what that tap added. After leaving the screen, remove from the day page instead.
