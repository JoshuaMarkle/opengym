Food v5: built from the design system

v5 keeps every screen and layout from v4 and rebuilds the details with design/proposals/design-system.html. Phones are drawn at 1:1 (375 × 812), so every size is the real px value. Type comes from the 12-step scale only: large titles at 34, detail titles at 30, rows at 17/15, meta at 14/13/12, caps labels at 11. Headers use the large title (tab roots) or the 52 px top bar. Meals and diets are folder rows: 52 px, Caps 11 label, a 16 px chevron box and children indented 24. The ring card is the doc's ring with legend (ring M 104 · stroke 8). Inputs are value boxes, inline inputs and steppers. Buttons are a filled 44 px primary, L discs (34), the dashed add and text buttons. Toasts, the keypad dock and the tab bar use the doc's specs, and every glyph is a drawn SVG. Targets: the diets are a plain list now, not a folder, and the active diet's check is blue again.

A · DAY PAGE

Meals fold, and the right one is open

Large title (Display 34) with the date in Footnote 13 and a 30 px "+" bar button. Below it: the week strip with S rings (36 · stroke 3), the target bar (h 8) and three labeled bars (h 4). Each meal is a folder row: the label is Caps 11, grey when closed and white when open, with the count in fg3 and the total as Body 15/600 + Caption 12 "kcal". Foods are dense two-line rows indented 24, and every meal ends in a 44 px "+ Add" row. The meal for the time of day opens itself: Breakfast until 11:00, Lunch until 4:00 pm, Dinner after that. Snacks only open when tapped.

S · SETUP

About you, goal, pace, result

Top bar, then a segmented step bar (h 4, gap 4: done blue, current white, to come surface-2). Questions are Title 1. Form rows are 52 px Body large with an inline input (32 · r8) or a compact segmented control on the right. Choices are two-line rows (60) with a 24 px check. T · TARGETS

Ring on top, diets below

The doc's L ring (144 · stroke 10, Title 3 in the middle) with the legend columns under it between hairlines. Under that, a Diets section label (Caps 11, with "protein / carbs / fat" as a Caption note) and a plain list. It doesn't fold. Each diet is a dense two-line row with an S ring (28 · stroke 3) in front and a 24 px check at the end. The active diet sits first with a blue check. Custom adds steppers (two 36 × 32 buttons) under each macro: + adds 2% to that macro and takes 1% from each of the other two.

C · ADD FOOD

Find it, set the amount, log it

The meal picker is the top-bar title (Headline 17 + ChevronDown 9). Search is 40 px. Shortcut tiles are 64 px on surface, and filters are 34 px chips. Results are dense two-line rows: a Caption detail with inline macros (each letter 700 in its color, numbers fg2), and an L disc (34) at the end. The scanner's result is a sheet: a Title 3 name, a Footnote source line, the ring card, a value box and the filled Log button.

D · SAVED MEALS

Saved meals and the Meals tab

D4 is built like the routine builder: Title 1 name, the ring card, rows with a drag handle at the end, and a dashed "+ Add food" button. In D5, tapping + logs the meal straight away and the disc fills blue with a check. Tapping it again takes the meal back off. A toast (48 · r14) confirms with Undo, 10 px above the bottom.
