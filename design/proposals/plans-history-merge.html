OpenGym · Proposal · Plans + History in one tab · Dark

One calendar for what you did and what's next

History and the plan calendar (2.20) both draw a month. This merges them: past days show what you logged, today and later show the plan, and missed days stand out. The tab bar drops to four tabs, Home · Calendar · Progress · Settings. Weeks start Sunday. Example plan: PPL weekly split (Mon Pull A · Tue Legs · Thu Push A · Fri Push B). Today is Thursday, Nov 12. Push B on Fri Nov 6 was missed, a Run was logged on the rest day Sat Nov 7, and Push B on Fri Nov 20 was moved to Sat 21. Every check uses the app's CheckGlyph SVG (the ✓ in the set table), not the text ✓. No dots: one workout a day is the normal case.

A · MONTH

Five ways to draw a day

Same data, same screen around it. Only the day cell changes. The range row gains Week in front of Month / 6M / Year / All.

B · WEEK

A week view

"Week" in the range row. It shows all seven days with the routine names written out, which a 7-column month can't fit.

C · TAP A DAY

Past, missed and future days

Tapping a day fills the panel under the calendar. Future months show only the plan, and a future day can be changed from there (the 2.21 sheet). Long-press does the same as before.

D · WHERE ROUTINES GO

The rest of the Plans tab

Plans also holds folders and routines that aren't on a schedule. With no Plans tab they need a home.

To decide
• Day cell: A1 (history, label under the number), A2 (outlines only), A3 (plan tiles), A4 or A5 (number and plan on one line)?
• Done days: show the check (A1, A4) or keep the routine code (A3, A5)? The check reads faster; the code says which workout.
• Week view: B1 (strip + list) or B2 (list only, like the History list)?
• Tab name: Calendar, Training or keep History? And where routines live: a third view in the header toggle (D1) or Home (D2)?
• Tapping a missed past day: offer "Log it late" and "Skip it", or only show it?
• Can you scroll to future months with no plan? Today the History arrow stops at this month; here it would stop at the end of the plan, or 3 months out.
