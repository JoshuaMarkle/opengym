Exercise popularity

Every built-in exercise and variation gets a popularity score from 0 to 10. Lists are ranked by it by default, specialist and rare ones are hidden until you search, the score itself is never shown, variation groups can grow (rows with every grip) without flooding the screen, and the score breaks close calls when focus lifts are picked automatically. Every table and list on this page is generated by exercise-popularity.py from src/db/seedExercises.ts.

C · FOCUS LIFTS

Popularity breaks close calls

Automatic focus lifts are today the 4 most-logged compound lifts (7.5, SPEC §13). Two changes: each lift's sessions are multiplied by 0.6 + 0.04 × popularity (0.60 for a 0, 1.00 for a 10), and a variation group gets at most one focus slot, so two pulldown grips can't take half of them. Example below: a lifter whose most-logged compounds are a Smith incline and a single-arm row.

Today: Smith Machine Incline Press, Single-Arm Cable Row, Lat Pulldown, Close-Grip Lat Pulldown. Proposed: Lat Pulldown, Bench Press, Smith Machine Incline Press, Leg Press. How it works

ScoreEvery built-in exercise has popularity, a whole number 0–10: how many lifters you'd expect to have it in a log. 10 is Bench Press; 9 is Squat, Deadlift, Lat Pulldown, Leg Press. It's a hand-set constant in the seed, never computed from your data. Custom exercises have none and count as 6.
HiddenScore ≤ 3 is hidden by default: left out of the Library and picker lists and of variation chips. It still shows (a) when you search for it, at the bottom under Rarely used, (b) whenever you've logged it or it's in a routine, (c) in a variation group's All list, under Rarely used, and (d) with Show hidden in the Library. No setting needed.
Never shownThe score is internal. No screen in the app shows a number, bar or label for it; it only decides order, hiding and focus-lift tie-breaks. (The bars on this board are for design work only.)
Library and picker orderPopular is the default sort: You do first (your 4 most-logged exercises; the picker keeps "In this routine" above it), then Most popular: every other visible exercise, most popular first, then A–Z. A–Z is one tap away and is remembered. Muscle chips filter both; Cardio moves to the end of the chips (All, Chest, Back, Legs, Shoulders, Arms, Core, Cardio).
Search orderMatches you've logged first, then by score, then hidden matches dimmed under Rarely used. Typing "row" puts Barbell Row and Seated Cable Row above Yates Row and Helms Row.
Variation chipsOn the exercise screen and while logging: this exercise, then the variations you've logged (most sessions first), then the most popular ones, up to 4 chips. The rest sit behind "All N", a sheet ranked by score with your sessions in blue. A variation you've logged always gets a chip, even past 4. Labels come from the whole group (variationLabels), so a chip's name never changes when the set of chips does.
Focus lifts (automatic)Unchanged inputs: compound lifts logged in the last 8 weeks that have a trend number. New: rank by sessions × (0.6 + 0.04 × popularity) instead of sessions alone, and take at most one lift per variation group. The multiplier runs 0.60–1.00, so a lift you log 1.7× as often always wins; popularity only decides close calls and ties (today a tie falls to whichever was logged last). Customised focus lifts are never touched.
Data modelSeedExercise gains popularity: number and optional area?: 'forearms' | 'lower back'. Exercise copies popularity at seed time (so a re-seed can update scores without touching user edits). Hidden is derived, never stored. Farmer's Carry needs a new type, weight_time: LBS and TIME columns, est. 1RM and records skip it, Progress charts the heaviest weight with time as the tiebreak. New exercises seed like any other (ex-<slug>); regrouping changes only variationGroupId on exercises the user hasn't relinked.
AreaMuscles stay the seven filters. area only adds a word to the meta line ("Arms · Forearms · dumbbell") and to search, so "forearm" or "lower back" finds the right exercises without a new chip.
