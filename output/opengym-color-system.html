Working rules

CONSISTENCY BEFORE DECORATION

Blue is the action anchor. Links, tabs, selected fields, completion rings, plates and strength trends share one blue. Filled actions use a deeper blue so white labels stay readable.

Cyan and gold identify nutrition. Protein is blue, carbs cyan, fat gold. The colors stay the same across daily bars, weekly stacks, legends and labels. Calories are white; over-target totals turn orange.

Status keeps its meaning. Green means improvement or success. Orange means attention, drops or a missed day. Red means destructive actions, errors or a downward comparison. Every status includes a word or glyph.

Text stays legible. White for values and names. Secondary gray for dates and units. Tertiary gray only for nonessential hints. Disabled gray only for unavailable controls.

Separate with spacing and hairlines. Keep the page black, rows flat, and overlays charcoal. Reserve blue tints for selected or completed states. Keep rounded chart tops and tabular digits.

One source of color. Components reference semantic tokens. Derived fills follow their parent color. Never add a slightly different hardcoded blue to a chart, badge, logo or focus ring.
